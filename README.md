## Project : Analysis of U.S. Domestic Flight Delays Between 2019 And 2025
This project analyses U.S. domestic flight departure delays between 2019 and 2025. The aim is to understand which factors influence departure delays, examining them from several angles (airport size, demographics, weather, etc.).

The analysis covers flights departing from 15 airports, selected to provide coverage across the United States and to include airports of varying sizes, defined by the number of enplanements recorded in 2024.

The complete methodology and analysis are available in my [Notion Portfolio](https://guttural-tarn-af3.notion.site/Analysis-of-U-S-Domestic-Flight-Delays-Between-2019-And-2025-f9b460e76ed083ee996681f5c91b46bf?pvs=74)
